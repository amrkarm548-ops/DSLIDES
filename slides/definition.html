What Is a Goiter?
Enlarged thyroid in the neck

LATIN · GUTTUR = “THROAT”

Enlargement of the thyroid gland — regardless of cause or function

WHEN IS THE THYROID ENLARGED?

ON EXAMINATION

Each lobe > terminal phalanx of the patient’s thumb

ON ULTRASOUND

Total volume > ~18 mL (women) · > 25 mL (men)
