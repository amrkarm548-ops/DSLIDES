03  ·  RISK FACTORS
Who Is at Risk? — Continued

Hormonal changes

Puberty, pregnancy & menopause ↑ hormone demand

Radiation exposure

Prior head/neck irradiation or nuclear fallout

Medications

Lithium · amiodarone · immunosuppressants

Dietary goitrogens

Excess cassava, cabbage, broccoli, soy — worse with iodine deficiency

Smoking

Thiocyanate in smoke interferes with iodine uptake
