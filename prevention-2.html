Prevention — Beyond Iodine

Limit goitrogenic foods

Raw cassava, very large amounts of cruciferous vegetables — especially in iodine-deficient areas

Screen high-risk groups

Pregnant women · endemic regions · lithium/amiodarone users · family history

Avoid unnecessary neck radiation

Use thyroid shielding when imaging

Early medical evaluation

Of any new neck swelling
