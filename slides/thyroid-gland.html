01  ·  INTRODUCTION
The Thyroid Gland
Butterfly-shaped thyroid gland

LOCATION

Anterior neck — just below the larynx

Wraps around the trachea

HORMONES

T4 (thyroxine) & T3 (triiodothyronine)

Regulate metabolism, growth & development

ALSO SECRETES

Calcitonin

Contributes to calcium homeostasis
